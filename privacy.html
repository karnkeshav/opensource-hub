Privacy note

Draft: review and complete this before the site goes public, and adjust it if you add analytics or Google sign-in.

What the apps do

The apps run on your device. Documents you create stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

What this website collects

  • Download counts. GitHub counts how many times each release file is downloaded. We don’t see who downloaded it.
  • App requests. If you send a request, we receive what you type. Name and email are optional and used only to reply.
  • Sign in with Google (optional). If you choose to sign in, Google shares your name, email address and a user ID with us. Signing in is never required to use the apps or download them. While signed in, we record which app you chose to download and when.
  • Where requests go. Requests are emailed to the site owner through a form service, and may also be stored in a database hosted by Google (Firebase).

Your choices

Ask us to delete a request you sent, or to delete your sign-in data, and we will. Contact details will be added here before launch.
